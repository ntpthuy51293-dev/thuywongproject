## Root causes, biggest first

1. **Interest rates (whole market).** The Iran war pushed oil above $90 and inflation to 3.4%. The 10-year yield went from >4.6% on 21 Jul ([CRFB](https://www.crfb.org/blogs/10-year-treasury-yield-eclipsed-46)) to 4.70% on 18 Aug ([Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-tuesday-august-18-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-080822735.html)) to 5.17% on 25 Sep ([Advisor Perspectives](https://www.advisorperspectives.com/dshort/updates/2026/07/31/treasury-yields-snapshot-july-31-2026)), its highest since 2007. On 16 Sep the Fed raised rates to 3.75-4.00%, its first hike since 2023, and signaled more ([CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/fed-interest-rate-decision-today/)).
2. **Doubts about AI financing and returns (sector).** On 28 Jul chip stocks entered a bear market. The triggers were reports that Nvidia was negotiating to backstop $250B of OpenAI data center funding, a 470% IPO debut by Chinese memory maker CXMT, and China mass-producing its own chip tools ([Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/micron-sk-hynix-stocks-sink-as-ai-chip-sell-off-deepens-125622548.html)). Nvidia later disclosed $108.5B of maximum guarantee exposure to customers ([Kiplinger](https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/live/nvidia-earnings-live-updates-and-commentary-august-2026)). Analysts called it "a repricing of valuation, financing costs and AI-return risk" rather than a demand collapse ([Aizigoo](https://aizigoo.com/en/now/ai-infrastructure-stocks-july-2026-selloff-outlook)).
3. **Valuation (sector).** Several stocks fell on strong results: FN beat on every line and fell 19%, STRL beat and fell 13%, and AMKR beat but fell 25% on a soft outlook.
4. **Margins and cash flow (company).** MOD's data center margins fell and its free cash flow turned negative ([Quiver](https://www.quiverquant.com/news/Modine+Manufacturing+falls+as+investors+appear+to+revisit+margin+and+execution+concerns)). FN's free cash flow for the year was $4M ([TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/fabrinets-q4-earnings-beat-on-every-line-so-why-did-the-stock-fall-19)). STRL's mix is shifting to lower-margin electrical work, and it is short of electricians ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/sterling-infrastructure-q2-2026-slides-90-revenue-jump-shares-fall-93CH-4835176)).
5. **Slowdown talk and local opposition (sector).** The 12 Sep call to slow AI model development hit suppliers on 14 Sep ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/12/anthropic-ai-amodei-pacing)). A YouGov poll found 63% of Americans oppose a new data center in their community ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/analysis/these-ai-stocks-sold-off-hardeven-as-their-outlooks-improved-200688167)). Disclosure: I'm made by Anthropic.

## Bottom line

The drops come mostly from the price of money and investor mood, not from lost orders. Order books are still growing at most of these companies. The warning signs are AGX's shrinking backlog and margins and cash flow at MOD and FN.

**What would turn them around:** the 10-year yield falling back below about 4.6%, oil easing, hyperscalers holding capex at late-October earnings, and margins recovering in the next reports.

**Not verified:** the cause of the 30 Jul bounce and of the 28 Aug drop; MOD's peak month.
